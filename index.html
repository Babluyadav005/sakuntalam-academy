The Academy

A focused environment for serious learning.

Sakuntalam Academy is built around clear concepts, individual attention, regular testing and disciplined classroom learning. Students learn face-to-face with faculty in an environment designed for consistent academic progress.

Mission

Quality education, individual attention and disciplined learning that helps students build strong fundamentals and confidence.

Vision

To become a trusted center of excellence for Classes 4–12 and future-focused JEE & NEET preparation.

OFFLINE • CLASSROOM

Learning that happens in the classroom.

Every program at Sakuntalam Academy is conducted offline. No online classes — students learn directly with teachers, ask doubts in person and participate in regular tests.

Location: Shanti Nagar, Dumra, Sitamarhi, Bihar — Behind ITI
